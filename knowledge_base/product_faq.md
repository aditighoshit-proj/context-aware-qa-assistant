# Product FAQ

## Account Setup
To create an account, provide a valid email address and verify it using the confirmation link. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Two-factor authentication is available in account settings and is recommended for all users.

## Subscription Plans
The product offers Basic, Plus, and Team plans. The Basic plan supports one workspace and up to three members. Plus supports up to ten members. Team supports up to 50 members and includes shared administration controls. Current prices are displayed on the pricing page and may change.

## Data Export
Workspace admins can export workspace records as CSV files from Settings → Data → Export. Exports are prepared asynchronously, and the admin receives an email when the file is ready. Only workspace admins can start an export.

## Support
Support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time, excluding public holidays. Users can contact support through the in-product Help menu. The typical first response target is one business day.
