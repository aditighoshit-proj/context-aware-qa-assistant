# Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Carried-forward leave must be used by March 31; any remaining carried-forward days expire after that date. Leave requests should be submitted to the employee's manager at least five working days in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. For an absence of more than two consecutive working days, employees should provide a medical certificate to Human Resources. Sick leave does not carry forward to the next calendar year.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Teams may designate specific collaboration days when employees are expected to work from the office. Remote work is not guaranteed and may be changed based on business needs.

## Parental Leave
Eligible employees who have completed at least 12 months of service may take 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Employees should notify Human Resources as early as practical.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the transaction. Receipts are required for any single expense above ₹1,000. Manager approval is required before reimbursement is processed.

## Learning and Development
Employees may request up to ₹20,000 per calendar year for approved job-related courses or certifications. Requests must be approved by the manager before the course is purchased. Reimbursement requires proof of successful completion and a valid receipt.
